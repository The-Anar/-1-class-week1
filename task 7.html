Simple Pancakes

What You Need

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon butter

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add the milk and egg, then mix until the batter is smooth.
  3. Heat a pan and melt the butter over medium heat.
  4. Warning: Be careful when handling the hot pan and melted butter.
  5. Pour some batter into the pan and cook until bubbles appear on the surface.
  6. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until golden brown.
  7. Serve the pancakes warm and enjoy them with your favorite toppings.
